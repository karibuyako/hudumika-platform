# Service Provider Application Specification

## Supported provider types

- Plumbing.
- Electrical.
- Home cleaning.
- Appliance and AC repair.
- Painting and decor.
- Carpentry and furniture.
- Moving and logistics.
- Laundry and ironing.
- Beauty and grooming.
- Pest control.
- Future locally approved professional services.

## Surfaces

- Provider web portal for profile, calendar, services, pricing, and earnings.
- Provider mobile app for booking alerts, navigation, customer communication, and job status.

## Provider onboarding

1. Select trade and service categories.
2. Submit identity, phone, city, service area, experience, and profile information.
3. Upload qualifications, licences, references, and portfolio images where required.
4. Define availability, response time, travel radius, and pricing.
5. Complete safety and service-standard training.
6. Operations verifies and approves the profile.
7. Provider publishes the profile and accepts bookings.

## Provider profile

- Name or business name.
- Verified status and verification date.
- Trades and sub-services.
- Service areas.
- Availability calendar.
- Starting price and price rules.
- Experience and qualifications.
- Portfolio images.
- Completed jobs.
- Ratings and reviews.
- Cancellation and response metrics.

## Booking flow

1. Provider receives a booking request.
2. Provider reviews service description, address, photos, time, and expected price.
3. Provider accepts, declines, or proposes a new time/price.
4. Customer confirms any change.
5. Provider marks en route, arrived, and started.
6. Provider records materials or scope changes with customer approval.
7. Provider marks job complete and uploads completion evidence if required.
8. Customer confirms or raises a dispute.
9. Backend releases provider payout.

## Provider safety and quality

- Masked customer contact.
- No off-platform payment prompts.
- Incident reporting.
- Job cancellation reason tracking.
- Customer and provider mutual ratings.
- Repeat quality complaints trigger manual review.

## Provider acceptance criteria

- Provider cannot appear in search before approval.
- Customer sees a full price range before booking.
- Provider cannot mark completion without required evidence for regulated categories.
- Disputed jobs pause payout automatically.
- Provider can export earnings and job history.
